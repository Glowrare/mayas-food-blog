## with Lime Crema and Pickled Onion

Roasted sweet potato cubes get crispy edges and a creamy center, which is exactly what you want against cumin-scented beans. Quick-pickled onion is optional only if you enjoy less interesting tacos.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 2 medium sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 can black beans, rinsed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 cup sour cream or yogurt
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp white vinegar
- 8 tortillas
- Cilantro and hot sauce
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss onion with vinegar and a pinch of salt. Set aside 10 minutes.
2. Roast sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp oil, chili powder, and salt at 220°C for 25 minutes.
3. Warm beans with cumin, remaining oil, and salt.
4. Stir lime into sour cream.
5. Fill tortillas with potatoes, beans, crema, pickled onion, and cilantro.
