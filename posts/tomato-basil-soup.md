## with a Grilled Cheese on the Side

I brown the tomato paste in olive oil before the tomatoes go in. That one minute is the difference between flat and full. Basil goes in at the end so it stays green.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cans whole tomatoes
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- 1 handful basil
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and black pepper
- Heavy cream, optional

## Method

1. Soften onion in oil. Add garlic and tomato paste and cook until the paste darkens.
2. Add tomatoes, broth, sugar, salt, and pepper. Simmer 20 minutes.
3. Stir in most of the basil. Blend until smooth.
4. Taste for salt. Swirl in a little cream if you want it softer.
5. Serve with the remaining basil torn over the top.
