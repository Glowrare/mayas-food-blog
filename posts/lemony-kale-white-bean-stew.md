## with Garlic, Chili, and Parmesan

This is the pot I make when I want something warm that still tastes clean. Lemon zest goes in at the end so the broth stays bright. A Parmesan rind is worth fishing out of the fridge if you have one.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 2 cans white beans, rinsed
- 1 bunch kale, stems removed, chopped
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 Parmesan rind (optional)
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- Grated Parmesan, to serve
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Sauté onion in 2 tbsp oil until soft. Add garlic and chili flakes.
2. Add beans, broth, kale, Parmesan rind, salt, and pepper. Simmer 15 minutes.
3. Remove the rind. Stir in lemon zest, juice, and remaining oil.
4. Serve with more Parmesan.
