## with Tomato, Feta, and Fresh Dill

Farro stays pleasantly chewy after baking, unlike rice that can turn mushy. I mix the filling on the stove, then give the peppers just enough oven time to slump without collapsing.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 4 large bell peppers, halved and seeded
- 1 cup farro
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can diced tomatoes, drained
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta
- 2 tbsp chopped dill
- 2 tbsp chopped parsley
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Cook farro in salted water until tender. Drain.
2. Sauté onion in oil until soft. Add garlic, then tomatoes, and cook 5 minutes.
3. Stir in farro, most of the feta, herbs, salt, and pepper.
4. Nestle pepper halves in a baking dish. Fill generously. Sprinkle remaining feta.
5. Bake at 200°C for 25 minutes, until the peppers are tender.
