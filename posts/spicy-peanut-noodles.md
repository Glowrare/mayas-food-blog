## with Cucumber, Carrot, and Herbs

Whisk the sauce in a bowl while the noodles boil. If it is too thick, pasta water or a spoon of lime juice loosens it. I like it spicy enough to notice.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 300g wheat noodles or spaghetti
- 1/3 cup peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp chili crisp or chili flakes
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1 carrot, julienned
- 1/2 cucumber, julienned
- Cilantro and crushed peanuts

## Method

1. Boil the noodles. Rinse briefly if you want them cooler.
2. Whisk peanut butter, soy, lime, maple, chili, garlic, and 3 tbsp warm water.
3. Toss noodles with the sauce.
4. Add carrot and cucumber.
5. Top with cilantro and peanuts.
