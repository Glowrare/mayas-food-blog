## with Lemon and Parmesan

Store-bought gnocchi crisp up like little dumplings if you skip the boiling water and start them in a dry-ish pan. Brown butter and sage do the rest. Lemon keeps it from tasting only rich.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 500g potato gnocchi
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 12 sage leaves
- 1 garlic clove, smashed
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1/3 cup grated Parmesan
- Black pepper
- Salt

## Method

1. Crisp gnocchi in a large skillet with 1 tbsp butter over medium-high heat, 6–8 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
2. Add remaining butter. When it foams and smells nutty, fry sage and garlic 30 seconds.
3. Return gnocchi. Toss with lemon, Parmesan, salt, and plenty of pepper.
4. Serve immediately, sage leaves on top.
