## with Lime and Cilantro

A square of dark chocolate sounds odd and then it makes sense. It rounds the chipotle without making the pot taste like dessert. I serve it with rice or crushed tortilla chips.

## Ingredients (_serves 6_)

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 red pepper, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 chipotle in adobo, minced
- 3 cans black beans, drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 15g dark chocolate
- Lime and cilantro, to finish

## Method

1. Cook onion and pepper in oil until soft. Add garlic and spices for 1 minute.
2. Stir in chipotle, beans, tomatoes, and broth.
3. Simmer 25 minutes, until thick.
4. Stir in chocolate until it disappears. Salt to taste.
5. Finish with lime juice and cilantro.
