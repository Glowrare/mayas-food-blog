## with Spinach and Lime

Jarred curry paste is welcome here. I am not building a curry from scratch on a Tuesday. The lime at the end keeps the coconut from feeling heavy.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp curry paste
- 1 can coconut milk
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained
- 2 cups spinach
- 1 lime, juiced
- Salt
- Rice, to serve
- Cilantro

## Method

1. Soften onion in oil. Stir in curry paste for 1 minute.
2. Add coconut milk and chickpeas. Simmer 15 minutes.
3. Fold in spinach until just wilted.
4. Add lime juice and salt.
5. Serve over rice with cilantro.
