## with Avocado, Lime, and Crisp Tortillas

I use leftover roast chicken when I have it and poached breasts when I do not. The broth is canned tomatoes, onion, and a dried chili if the cupboard has one.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 2 chicken breasts
- 1 onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 can black beans, drained
- 4 corn tortillas, sliced
- Oil, for frying
- Avocado, lime, cilantro, and cheese, to serve

## Method

1. Simmer chicken in broth until cooked, about 15 minutes. Shred it and keep the broth.
2. Soften onion. Add garlic and chili powder, then tomatoes and the broth.
3. Simmer 10 minutes. Stir in beans and shredded chicken.
4. Fry tortilla strips in a little oil until crisp. Salt them.
5. Serve the soup with avocado, lime, cilantro, cheese, and the tortillas.
