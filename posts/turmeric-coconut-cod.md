## with Baby Spinach and Jasmine Rice

Poaching fish in coconut milk keeps it from drying out, which is useful on nights I cannot watch the stove. Turmeric and lime make the broth golden and sharp. Spoon it over rice so nothing is wasted.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 4 cod fillets
- 1 can coconut milk
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 lime, juiced
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 red chili, sliced (optional)
- Salt
- Jasmine rice, to serve

## Method

1. Warm coconut oil. Sauté garlic, ginger, and turmeric 1 minute.
2. Pour in coconut milk and a pinch of salt. Simmer 3 minutes.
3. Nestle in the cod. Cover and cook 6–8 minutes, until it flakes.
4. Stir in spinach and lime. Top with chili and serve over rice.
