## with Thyme and Parmesan

I brown the mushrooms separately so they stay meaty, then fold them in at the end. The rice should ripple when you shake the pan, not sit in a puddle.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 1/2 cups arborio rice
- 400g mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 4 cups warm vegetable broth
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 2 thyme sprigs
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Brown mushrooms in 1 tbsp butter. Season and set aside.
2. Soften shallot in remaining butter. Add rice and stir until the edges look clear.
3. Add wine and let it cook off. Add broth a ladle at a time, stirring, until the rice is tender.
4. Fold in mushrooms, parmesan, and thyme leaves.
5. Rest 2 minutes. It should be loose enough to spread on a plate.
