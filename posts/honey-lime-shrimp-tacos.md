## with Cabbage Slaw and Avocado

Shrimp cook in minutes, which is exactly why they belong on a Tuesday. The glaze is honey, lime, garlic, and a little chili. Keep the slaw sharp so the tacos stay bright instead of sweet.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 500g large shrimp, peeled
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 limes, juiced and zested
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cups shredded cabbage
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 8 small corn tortillas
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss shrimp with honey, lime zest, half the lime juice, garlic, chili powder, and salt.
2. Mix cabbage with remaining lime juice, cilantro, a pinch of salt, and 1 tbsp oil.
3. Sear shrimp in the remaining oil over high heat, 1–2 minutes per side, until glazed.
4. Warm tortillas. Fill with slaw, shrimp, and avocado.
