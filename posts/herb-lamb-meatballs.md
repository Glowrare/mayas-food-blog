## with Cucumber Yogurt

Baking on a rack, or just on a lined tray, gives you browning without standing at the stove. I serve these with warm flatbread or over rice.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 500g ground lamb
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley and mint
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1/2 cucumber, grated and squeezed
- Salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Mix lamb, herbs, spices, garlic, breadcrumbs, egg, and salt.
2. Roll into meatballs and set on a lined tray.
3. Bake 18 minutes, until browned.
4. Stir cucumber into yogurt with a pinch of salt.
5. Serve the meatballs with the yogurt and extra herbs.
