## with Sweet Peppers and Red Onion

Chicken thighs forgive a busy evening. A three-spice rub and a crowded pan of peppers is all the seasoning they need. Serve with rice or crusty bread to catch the smoky drippings.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- 2 red peppers, sliced
- 2 yellow peppers, sliced
- 1 large red onion, wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- Salt and black pepper
- Chopped parsley

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Pat the thighs dry.
2. Mix paprika, garlic powder, cumin, 1 tsp salt, and pepper. Rub over the chicken.
3. Toss peppers and onion with oil and a pinch of salt. Spread in a roasting pan and nestle in the thighs, skin up.
4. Roast 35–40 minutes, until the skin is crisp and juices run clear.
5. Splash vinegar over the vegetables and scatter parsley.
