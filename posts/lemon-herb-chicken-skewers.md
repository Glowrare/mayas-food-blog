## with Herbed Yogurt

Thighs stay juicy under a broiler in a way breasts do not. If you have ten minutes in the morning, the marinade can sit all day. If you do not, even twenty minutes helps.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 700g boneless chicken thighs, cubed
- 2 lemons, zested and juiced
- 3 garlic cloves, grated
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tbsp chopped dill
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Toss chicken with lemon zest, half the juice, garlic, oregano, 2 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Thread onto skewers. Broil 10–12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.
3. Stir yogurt with dill, remaining lemon juice, and a pinch of salt.
4. Rest the skewers 3 minutes.
5. Serve with the yogurt and a pile of greens or warm pita.
