## with Lemon and Parmesan

A peeler makes the ribbons. They only need a minute in the hot pasta so they stay green and a little crisp, not watery.

## Ingredients (_serves 3_)

- 300g spaghetti
- 2 zucchini, peeled into ribbons
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 garlic clove, sliced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- Chili flakes
- Salt
- Basil

## Method

1. Boil spaghetti in salted water. Save pasta water.
2. Warm oil and garlic until fragrant. Add zucchini ribbons and a pinch of salt.
3. Toss 1 minute, until just softened.
4. Add pasta, lemon zest, lemon juice, and a splash of pasta water.
5. Take off the heat. Fold in parmesan and basil.
