## with Herbs, Celery, and Yogurt

Rotisserie chicken is fair game. The dressing is yogurt, lemon, and whatever soft herbs are in the fridge, blended until it is the color of a lawn.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 3 cups shredded cooked chicken
- 2 celery stalks, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 cup mixed herbs (dill, parsley, chives)
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 small garlic clove
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt
- Butter lettuce, to serve

## Method

1. Blend yogurt, herbs, lemon, garlic, oil, and salt until smooth and green.
2. Toss chicken and celery with most of the dressing.
3. Taste and add salt.
4. Spoon into lettuce cups or over torn greens.
5. Drizzle the remaining dressing and add extra herbs.
