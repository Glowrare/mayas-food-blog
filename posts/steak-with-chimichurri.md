## with Parsley, Garlic, and Vinegar

I make more chimichurri than the steak needs. It keeps for a few days and wakes up eggs, beans, or tomorrow’s roast vegetables.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 2 steaks (ribeye or sirloin)
- 1 cup parsley leaves
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Chop parsley and garlic. Stir with chili, vinegar, oil, and salt.
2. Salt the steaks and let them sit while the pan gets very hot.
3. Sear 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, or to your liking.
4. Rest 5 minutes.
5. Slice and spoon chimichurri over the top.
