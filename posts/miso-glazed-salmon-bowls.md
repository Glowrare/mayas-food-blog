## with Cucumber, Avocado, and Rice

The glaze is white miso, maple, and a splash of soy, brushed on in the last few minutes so it caramelizes instead of burning. I keep the vegetables cold and the rice warm. That contrast is the whole point.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 2 salmon fillets
- 1 cup short-grain rice
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 scallions, sliced
- Sesame seeds

## Method

1. Cook the rice. Stir miso, maple, soy, and vinegar into a glaze.
2. Heat a nonstick pan over medium. Cook salmon skin-side down for 4 minutes.
3. Flip, brush with glaze, and cook 2–3 minutes more until lacquered.
4. Divide rice into bowls. Top with salmon, cucumber, and avocado.
5. Scatter scallions and sesame seeds. Spoon over any glaze left in the pan.
