## with Lemon, Pecorino, and Chili

High heat is what makes broccoli a main, not a side. I roast it until the florets are almost burnt, then fold in beans while the pan is still hot so they pick up the oil and lemon.

## Ingredients (_serves 3_)

- 2 heads broccoli, cut into florets
- 1 can cannellini beans, rinsed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1/3 cup shaved pecorino
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Toss broccoli with 2 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 230°C for 18–20 minutes, until charred.
2. Whisk remaining oil with lemon juice, zest, garlic, and chili.
3. Toss hot broccoli with beans and dressing.
4. Finish with pecorino.
