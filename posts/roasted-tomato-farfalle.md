## with Garlic and Basil

High heat, not a gentle roast. You want jammy tomatoes and a few blackened edges. Farfalle catches the sauce in its folds better than long noodles do.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 700g cherry tomatoes
- 5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 350g farfalle
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 handful basil
- Salt
- Parmesan, optional

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss tomatoes and garlic with oil, chili, and salt.
2. Roast 20 minutes, until the tomatoes burst.
3. Boil farfalle in salted water. Save a cup of pasta water.
4. Toss pasta with the tomatoes and enough pasta water to make a sauce.
5. Tear in basil. Add parmesan if you like.
