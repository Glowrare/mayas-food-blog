## with Cucumber, Scallions, and Sesame

Leftover rice is the point, not a compromise. Chili crisp in the pan perfumes the oil before the eggs go in, so the whites blister and the yolks stay runny. Cool cucumber keeps it from being only spicy.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 3 cups cooked rice, warmed
- 4 eggs
- 2 tbsp chili crisp
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss cucumber with vinegar and a pinch of salt.
2. Warm chili crisp in a nonstick skillet. Fry eggs until the edges crisp and yolks stay jammy.
3. Divide rice between bowls. Splash with soy sauce.
4. Top with eggs, cucumber, scallions, extra chili crisp, and sesame seeds.
