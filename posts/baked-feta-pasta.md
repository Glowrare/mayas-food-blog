## with Burst Tomatoes and Basil

Use a block of feta, not crumbles. The block melts into a sauce instead of drying out. Chili and garlic go on the tray so they roast with the tomatoes.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 700g cherry tomatoes
- 200g block of feta
- 3 garlic cloves
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 350g short pasta
- 1 handful basil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Put feta in the middle of a baking dish and surround it with tomatoes and garlic.
2. Drizzle with oil, chili, salt, and pepper. Bake 30 minutes, until the tomatoes burst.
3. Boil the pasta in salted water.
4. Mash the feta and tomatoes into a sauce. Toss with pasta and a splash of pasta water.
5. Tear basil over the top.
