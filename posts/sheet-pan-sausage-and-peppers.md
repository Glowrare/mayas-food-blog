## with Red Onion and Crusty Bread

I use whatever sausages are good at the shop. Italian fennel is my default. A splash of vinegar at the end cuts the richness, and bread is not optional.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 6 fresh sausages
- 3 bell peppers, sliced
- 1 red onion, wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp fennel seeds
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- Salt and black pepper
- Crusty bread, to serve

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss peppers and onion with oil, fennel, salt, and pepper.
2. Nestle the sausages among the vegetables.
3. Roast 25–30 minutes, turning the sausages once.
4. Splash vinegar over the tray.
5. Serve with bread to catch the juices.
