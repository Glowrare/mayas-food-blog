## with Turmeric, Ginger, and Cilantro

Dal is the pot I start when I need dinner to simmer while I do something else. Red lentils cook fast and break down into a stew that tastes like it took longer. A finishing swirl of coconut milk and lemon is non-negotiable.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 1/2 cups red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can coconut milk
- 3 cups water or broth
- 1 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- Cilantro and rice, to serve
- Salt

## Method

1. Sauté onion in coconut oil until golden. Add ginger, garlic, and spices for 1 minute.
2. Stir in lentils, tomatoes, most of the coconut milk, water, and salt.
3. Simmer 20–25 minutes, stirring, until thick and creamy.
4. Finish with remaining coconut milk and lemon. Top with cilantro.
