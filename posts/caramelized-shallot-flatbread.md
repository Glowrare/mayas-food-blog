## with Ricotta, Thyme, and Honey

Shallots take patience, not skill. Let them go jammy, then spread them over stretched dough with dollops of ricotta. A drizzle of honey after baking is the move that makes people ask for the recipe.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 1 ball pizza dough
- 4 large shallots, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil, plus more for the crust
- 1 tsp balsamic vinegar
- 3/4 cup ricotta
- 1 tsp thyme leaves
- 1 tsp honey
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Cook shallots in oil over medium-low heat 15–20 minutes, until jammy. Stir in balsamic and salt.
2. Stretch dough on a sheet pan. Brush with oil.
3. Scatter shallots. Add ricotta in spoonfuls and sprinkle thyme.
4. Bake at 240°C for 12–14 minutes, until the edges blister.
5. Finish with honey and black pepper.
