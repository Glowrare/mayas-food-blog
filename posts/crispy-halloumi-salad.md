## with Tomatoes, Cucumber, and Grains

Dry the halloumi or it will not brown. I use leftover quinoa or farro, whatever is in the container from earlier in the week.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 225g halloumi, sliced
- 2 cups cooked grains
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cucumber, sliced
- 1 handful mint
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lemon, juiced
- Salt

## Method

1. Pat halloumi dry. Sear in a dry nonstick pan until deep golden on both sides.
2. Toss grains, tomatoes, cucumber, and mint with 1 tbsp oil and most of the lemon.
3. Salt the vegetables lightly. Halloumi is already salty.
4. Lay the halloumi over the salad.
5. Finish with the remaining oil and lemon.
