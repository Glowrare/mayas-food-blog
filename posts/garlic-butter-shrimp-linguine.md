## with Chili and Parsley

I pull the pasta a minute early and finish it in the shrimp butter so the noodles drink up the sauce. A pinch of chili keeps it from tasting only rich.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 300g linguine
- 400g shrimp, peeled
- 4 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil linguine in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Save a cup of pasta water.
2. Melt butter. Cook garlic and chili until fragrant, then add shrimp.
3. Cook shrimp 2 minutes, until pink. Add lemon zest and juice.
4. Toss in the pasta with a splash of pasta water until glossy.
5. Finish with parsley and more lemon if you want it brighter.
