## with Garlic Soy Sauce and Rice

Slice the steak against the grain while it is still a little cold so the pieces stay thin. Broccoli gets a head start in the pan, then the beef goes in last. The sauce is pantry staples whisked in a mug.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 450g flank steak, thinly sliced
- 1 large head broccoli, florets
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- Sesame seeds
- Rice, to serve

## Method

1. Whisk soy, sugar, vinegar, cornstarch, and 2 tbsp water.
2. Stir-fry broccoli in 1 tbsp oil with a splash of water until bright and tender-crisp. Set aside.
3. Sear beef in remaining oil in batches. Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
4. Return broccoli. Pour in sauce and toss until thick and shiny.
5. Sprinkle sesame seeds. Serve with rice.
