## with Snap Peas and Jasmine Rice

The trick is drying the tofu and giving it space in the pan. Once the edges are golden, the sauce takes less than a minute. I keep a jar of the sauce mix in the fridge for nights when chopping is all I can manage.

## Ingredients (_serves 3_)

- 400g extra-firm tofu, cubed
- 2 cups snap peas
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 tsp sesame seeds
- Jasmine rice, to serve

## Method

1. Pat tofu very dry. Sear in 1 tbsp oil until golden on most sides. Set aside.
2. Stir-fry snap peas and pepper in remaining oil for 2 minutes.
3. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 30 seconds.
4. Return tofu. Pour in soy, vinegar, maple, and sesame oil. Toss until glossy.
5. Sprinkle sesame seeds and serve over rice.
