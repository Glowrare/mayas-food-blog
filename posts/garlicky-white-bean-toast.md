## with Lemon, Chili, and Olive Oil

Mash half the beans so they stay on the bread. A raw garlic hit at the end, plus lemon, keeps it from tasting like cafeteria beans.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 4 thick slices of bread
- 1 can white beans, drained
- 2 garlic cloves, one sliced and one grated
- 2 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish
- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes
- Salt
- Parsley or arugula

## Method

1. Toast the bread until the edges are dark.
2. Warm sliced garlic in oil. Add beans, chili, and a splash of water.
3. Mash half the beans and stir. Season with salt and lemon.
4. Take off the heat and stir in the grated garlic.
5. Pile onto toast. Finish with oil and herbs.
