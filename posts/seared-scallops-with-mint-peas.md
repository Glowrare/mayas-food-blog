## with Brown Butter and Lemon

Pat the scallops very dry or they will steam. The peas can be frozen. Butter, lemon, and a handful of mint turn them into a sauce that tastes like spring even in March.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 8 large sea scallops, patted dry
- 2 cups frozen peas
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small shallot, minced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 8 mint leaves, chopped
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Season scallops with salt. Warm peas with shallot and 1 tbsp butter until just tender.
2. Smash half the peas. Stir in lemon zest, mint, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat oil in a skillet until shimmering. Sear scallops 90 seconds per side.
4. Add remaining butter and lemon juice to the pan and baste once.
5. Spoon peas onto plates and set the scallops on top.
