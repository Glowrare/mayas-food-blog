## with Orange, Thyme, and Pan Juices

Sunday roast energy without a long ingredient list. Citrus in the cavity and under the bird perfume the drippings. I spatchcock when I am impatient; otherwise a regular roast works just as well.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 whole chicken (about 1.6kg)
- 2 oranges, sliced
- 1 onion, thick slices
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 small bunch thyme
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- Black pepper
- 1/2 cup chicken broth

## Method

1. Heat oven to 220°C. Pat the chicken dry. Rub with oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Scatter orange, onion, and thyme in a roasting pan. Set the chicken on top. Stuff the cavity with lemon.
3. Roast 55–70 minutes, until the thickest part of the thigh hits 74°C.
4. Rest 10 minutes. Deglaze the pan with broth and spoon the juices over slices.
