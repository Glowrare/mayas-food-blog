## with Brown Butter Sage

Roasting the squash first gives the soup a caramel edge that boiling never does. The fried sage is not a garnish I skip. It smells like the whole kitchen.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 medium butternut squash, cubed
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 8 sage leaves
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Toss squash and onion with oil and salt. Roast at 220°C for 25 minutes.
2. Simmer the roasted vegetables in broth for 10 minutes. Add nutmeg.
3. Blend until completely smooth. Season again.
4. Fry sage in butter until crisp. Pour the brown butter over the soup.
5. Serve with the sage leaves on top.
