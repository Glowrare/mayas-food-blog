## with Roasted Green Beans

Pork tenderloin is weeknight-friendly if you do not overcook it. Maple and grainy mustard give you a glaze in one bowl. Pull the meat at 63°C and let it rest so it stays blush in the center.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 2 pork tenderloins (about 1kg total)
- 400g green beans, trimmed
- 2 tbsp grainy mustard
- 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp chopped rosemary
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Mix mustard, maple, vinegar, rosemary, 1 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper.
2. Toss green beans with remaining oil and salt. Spread on a sheet pan.
3. Sear tenderloins in a skillet 2 minutes per side, then brush with glaze.
4. Roast everything at 220°C for 12–15 minutes, until the pork hits 63°C.
5. Rest 5 minutes. Slice and drizzle with pan juices.
