## with Spinach and Basil

This is pantry cooking at its best. Orzo drinks tomato and broth until the skillet looks creamy even before the splash of milk. I use this when I want pasta but do not want a second pot for sauce.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 1/2 cups orzo
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 1/4 cup milk or oat milk
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
- Fresh basil
- Salt and chili flakes

## Method

1. Sauté onion in oil until translucent. Add garlic and oregano.
2. Stir in orzo, tomatoes, broth, salt, and chili flakes. Simmer, stirring often, 12–14 minutes.
3. Fold in spinach, milk, and Parmesan until creamy.
4. Top with basil and more cheese.
