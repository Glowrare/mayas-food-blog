## with Toasted Cumin and Lemon

Blender soups can taste steamed. Roasting the cauliflower and onion first is the fix. Tahini adds body, and a squeeze of lemon at the end keeps it from feeling heavy.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 1 large cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 yellow onion, wedges
- 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 1 lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper
- Chili flakes, to serve

## Method

1. Toss cauliflower, onion, and garlic with 2 tbsp oil, cumin, salt, and pepper. Roast at 220°C for 30 minutes.
2. Squeeze the garlic from its skins. Blend vegetables with broth and tahini until smooth.
3. Warm in a pot. Add lemon juice and remaining oil. Taste for salt.
4. Ladle into bowls and finish with chili flakes.
