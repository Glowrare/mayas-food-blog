## with Egg, Peas, and Scallions

Day-old rice is the secret. Fresh rice clumps. I spread leftovers on a tray while the shrimp cooks, then the whole thing comes together in one wok.

## Ingredients (_serves 3_)

- 3 cups cold cooked rice
- 300g shrimp, peeled
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil. Scramble the eggs and set them aside.
2. Add remaining oil and sear shrimp until just pink. Remove.
3. Stir-fry peas and rice until the grains separate and sound dry.
4. Return shrimp and eggs. Add soy, sesame oil, and scallions.
5. Toss 30 seconds more and serve immediately.
