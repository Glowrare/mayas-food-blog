## with Yogurt and Herbs

Harissa varies in heat, so I start with a tablespoon and taste the oil before it goes on the carrots. Couscous only needs a kettle and a lid.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 700g carrots, cut into sticks
- 1 tbsp harissa
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups couscous
- 1 1/2 cups boiling water
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 handful herbs
- Salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss carrots with harissa, oil, and salt.
2. Roast 25 minutes, shaking once, until browned at the tips.
3. Cover couscous with boiling water and a pinch of salt. Lid on for 5 minutes, then fluff.
4. Stir lemon into the yogurt.
5. Serve carrots over couscous with yogurt and herbs.
