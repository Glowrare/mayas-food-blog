## with Thyme and Lemon

Do not stir the rice once the lid goes on. The crust at the bottom is a feature. A squeeze of lemon at the table wakes the whole pot up.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 6 bone-in chicken thighs
- 1 1/2 cups long-grain rice
- 1 onion, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 1/2 cups chicken broth
- 1 lemon
- 4 thyme sprigs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Salt the chicken. Brown it in oil, skin-side down, until deep golden. Set aside.
2. Soften onion in the drippings. Add garlic, rice, and thyme. Stir 1 minute.
3. Pour in broth and the zest of the lemon. Nestle the chicken on top.
4. Cover and simmer 25 minutes, until the rice is tender.
5. Rest 5 minutes. Finish with lemon juice.
