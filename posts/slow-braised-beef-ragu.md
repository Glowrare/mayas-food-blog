## with Pappardelle

This is the recipe I start when I am home for the afternoon. The meat should fall apart when you look at it. A knob of butter at the end makes the sauce cling.

## Ingredients (_serves 6_)

- 900g beef shoulder, cubed
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 3 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup red wine
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt
- Pappardelle and parmesan, to serve

## Method

1. Salt the beef and brown it in oil. Set aside.
2. Cook onion and carrot until soft. Add garlic and tomato paste.
3. Pour in wine and let it reduce by half. Add tomatoes, broth, bay, and the beef.
4. Cover and braise at 160°C for 2 1/2 hours, until shreddable.
5. Shred the beef into the sauce and toss with pappardelle and parmesan.
