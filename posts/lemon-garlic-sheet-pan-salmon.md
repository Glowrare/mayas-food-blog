## with Roasted Asparagus and Baby Potatoes

A hot sheet pan does the heavy lifting here. Salmon stays juicy under a lemon-garlic glaze while potatoes crisp and asparagus picks up just enough char. It is the kind of dinner I make when I want something green, filling, and almost no dishes.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 4 salmon fillets (about 150g each)
- 500g baby potatoes, halved
- 1 bunch asparagus, trimmed
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Toss potatoes with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 15 minutes.
2. Whisk remaining oil with garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice, mustard, salt, and pepper.
3. Push potatoes to one side. Add salmon and asparagus. Spoon the sauce over the fish.
4. Roast 10–12 minutes, until the salmon flakes and asparagus is tender-crisp.
5. Finish with parsley and extra lemon.
