## with Thyme and Cracked Pepper

Miso gives this pan sauce the depth that usually takes stock and time. I brown the mushrooms hard so they actually taste like mushrooms, then melt in butter and white miso off the heat so nothing turns grainy.

## Ingredients (_serves 2_)

- 250g spaghetti
- 400g mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
- Black pepper
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Save 3/4 cup pasta water.
2. Sear mushrooms in oil over high heat until deeply browned. Season with salt.
3. Lower the heat. Add garlic and thyme for 30 seconds.
4. Stir miso into the butter, then toss with pasta, mushrooms, pasta water, and Parmesan.
5. Finish with a lot of black pepper.
