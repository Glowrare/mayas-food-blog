## with Orzo, Greens, and Lemon

Poaching the meatballs in the broth keeps them tender and seasons the pot at the same time. Orzo cooks right in the soup, so there is only one pan to wash.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 450g ground turkey
- 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 3/4 cup orzo
- 2 cups chopped spinach
- 1 carrot, diced
- 1 lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper
- Parmesan, to serve

## Method

1. Mix turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, oregano, salt, and pepper. Roll into small meatballs.
2. Bring broth to a simmer with the carrot.
3. Add meatballs and cook 8 minutes.
4. Stir in orzo and simmer until tender, about 10 minutes.
5. Add spinach and lemon. Serve with parmesan.
