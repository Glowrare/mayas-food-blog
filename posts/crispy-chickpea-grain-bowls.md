## with Lemony Quinoa and Tahini Yogurt

These bowls are built for leftovers. Roast a tray of chickpeas until they rattle in the pan, cook a pot of quinoa, and pack vegetables that stay crisp. The sauce is just yogurt, tahini, lemon, and a little maple.

## Ingredients (_serves 4_)

- 2 cans chickpeas, drained and dried
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- 2 cups shredded kale
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss chickpeas with oil, paprika, cumin, and salt. Roast at 220°C for 25 minutes until crisp.
2. Cook quinoa in salted water. Fluff and stir in half the lemon juice.
3. Massage kale with a pinch of salt until it darkens and softens.
4. Whisk yogurt, tahini, remaining lemon, maple, and a splash of water.
5. Divide quinoa, vegetables, and chickpeas into bowls. Drizzle generously.
